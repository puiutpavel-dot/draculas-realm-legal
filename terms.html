1. The app

Dracula's Realm is a travel guide to Transylvania and the history and legend of Vlad III Dracula. It is provided by Pavel Puiuț, Romania (“we”). The app is free and requires no account.

2. Historical content

Stories, facts and legends are provided for information and entertainment. We separate documented history from legend wherever we can, but historical sources are often uncertain. Opening hours, access and prices of the sites can change: please check them locally before you travel.

3. Tours and quote requests

  • Tour descriptions and prices in the app are indicative.
  • Sending a quote request is free and does not create a booking, a contract or any payment. No payment is taken in the app.
  • We reply by email, usually within 24 hours, with availability and a final offer.
  • If you accept an offer, the tour is organised and invoiced by a licensed Romanian travel agency, named in the offer. The booking contract, payment terms, cancellation and refund conditions and insurance are those of that agency and are sent to you before you pay. Your rights under the EU Package Travel Directive apply to that contract.

4. Safety

  • Do not use the app while driving. The audio guide is designed to play without touching the screen; let a passenger handle the phone.
  • Some sites involve steep climbs (for example the 1,480 steps to Poenari Citadel), mountain roads that close in winter (Transfăgărășan) or areas with wild animals, including bears. Follow local signs and instructions.
  • Check-in badges are a game: never enter closed or private areas to unlock them.
