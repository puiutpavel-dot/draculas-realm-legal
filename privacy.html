Privacy Policy

Dracula's Realm (iOS app) · Last updated: 2 October 2026

Who we are

Dracula's Realm is developed by Pavel Puiuț, Romania (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described below. Contact: puiutpavel@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your location is used only on your iPhone and is never sent to us.
  • We only receive personal data when you choose to send us a tour quote request by email.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location to show your position on the map, the distance to each site, to unlock badges when you check in, and – only while you switch the Audio guide on – to play stories automatically as you arrive at a site, also in the background. Location is processed on your device only. It is not stored by us, not sent to any server and not shared. You can turn location access off at any time in iOS Settings.

Data stored on your device

Places you save, badges you earn and the quote requests you send are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also reset it in the app under Settings.

Tour quote requests

When you request a quote for a tour, the app prepares an email with the details you entered: your name, email address, phone number, country, notes, the tour, dates and number of travellers. The email is sent from your own email app, only when you press Send. We use this information only to answer your request and prepare an offer (legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

If you decide to book, the tour is organised by a licensed Romanian travel agency, which will be named in our offer. Your details are shared with that agency only to organise the tour you asked for. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing.

We keep quote request emails for up to 2 years, unless a booking requires longer retention by law, and delete them earlier on request.

Maps and photos

Maps are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit), under Apple's privacy policy. Photos are bundled in the app (from Wikimedia Commons, credited in the app) and do not load any third-party trackers.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability. Write to us at the address above. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro) or the authority in your country.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We will publish any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
